01 — About

The story so far

I started on the engineering side. At NPCI, I worked on the infrastructure behind India's national payments system, simulating 25,000+ concurrent transactions and automating anomaly detection that replaced 3 manual analyst roles. At ArisGlobal, I built a BI product for pharma clients like J&J and AstraZeneca that every VP in the company adopted, saving $150K a year and earning a Spot Award.

Over time I cared less about how things broke and more about why we built them that way. So I moved into product. As a Technical Product Manager Intern at Marketeq Digital, I owned a checkout flow end to end: 87+ user stories and 50+ edge cases, from spec to release.

Now I'm building AI products. Forensic-AI reads SEC filings and flags risk with cited evidence. In finance, an AI that is confidently wrong is worse than no AI at all.
